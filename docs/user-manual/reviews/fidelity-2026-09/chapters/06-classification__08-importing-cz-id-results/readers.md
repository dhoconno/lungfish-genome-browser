## Consensus

Thirty-five rows were hit by three or more readers.

- Kraken 2 is named as a comparison point with nothing saying it is a classifier LGE runs itself.
- The test-fixture repository path is quoted as the source of the chapter's numbers with no note that readers cannot and need not open it.
- Nothing tells readers how to check their own exported file for the three required columns.
- The warning under step 5 is a forward reference readers must jump ahead for and then find their place again.
- The equivalent command line in the Operations row is never marked as optional for app-only readers.
- The long clause about which characters get replaced by a hyphen is hard to parse on a first read.
- The Settings section says it has no settings and then describes three flags at length.
- Argv, exit status, and wall time appear together with none of the three glossed.
- The kreport's six-column block leaves its second and third columns unlabelled.
- The 88 of 1,200 reads sentence draws several different kinds of confusion about the arithmetic and the source of the numbers.
- NT, NR, percent identity, alignment length, and e-value are used repeatedly without ever being expanded or glossed.
- The sunburst chart cannot be pictured from the prose alone, with no figure filled in.
- The "3 taxa for two real ones" count reads as a bug rather than as expected behavior.
- BLAST Verify and Export are named with no explanation of what either does.
- The known-defect note cites a source file and line numbers in text written for readers.
- The command-line section is never marked optional at its start.
- The RPM value of 73333.0 is never judged as high, low, or ordinary.
- The two similarly worded CLI commands differ only in word order and are easy to mistake for each other.
- The root row is never introduced before it appears carrying the sample's total read count.
- Reference database is never glossed at its first use.
- The rhetorical question "So what should you do with this?" breaks the reader's flow before answering itself.
- The .lungfishtax extension appears a sentence before the word bundle is glossed.
- Readers cannot judge what to do with a checksum once the concept is introduced.
- "The long tail of one-read hits" is jargon with no plain-language equivalent.
- The sentence naming this as one of the manual's viral examples reads as an aside to the manual's editors, not to the reader.
- Plugin pack is never glossed even while readers are told they do not need one.
- Drag-and-drop is offered as an alternative with no confirmation that it completes the import the same way.
- Readers cannot tell whether the Operations Panel opens on its own when an import starts.
- Placeholder paths like `/path/to/...` are never flagged as placeholders.
- The truncated checksum value gives readers nothing to compare it against.
- Clade is glossed in a way that fights the sunburst's own outward-radiating layout.
- "A count far smaller than expected" gives no number to judge against.
- The root-row-mismatch check names a cause but no remedy.
- The JSON output's camelCase field names are never explained.
- Step 5's heading never says the command-line step is optional.
