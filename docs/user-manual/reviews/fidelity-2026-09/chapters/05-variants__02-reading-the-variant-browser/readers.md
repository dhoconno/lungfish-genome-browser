## Consensus

Twenty-five rows were hit by three or more readers.

- The 250-point drawer height is given in a unit no reader recognized.
- HG002 is never explained as a known reference sample.
- Plugin pack is used with no gloss and no statement of whether the reader already has one.
- The Operations panel is introduced with no description of what a finished or running row looks like.
- The seven standard VCF columns cannot be reconciled with the six rows shown in the table.
- iVar is named once with no gloss and no clear statement of whether it matters for this fixture.
- The DP4, MQ, AC, and SB abbreviations are never expanded.
- The haploid-only logic for the frequency chips is never explained and reads as backwards.
- The scope setting behind the 574-row worked example is explained only in Settings, not alongside the example.
- The genome track of tick marks is never introduced before the chapter warns about its colors.
- The 623 plus 415 genotype count does not match the 1,056 total, and the missing eighteen rows are never named.
- The "one difference every 470 bases" arithmetic cannot be checked or judged against the one-in-a-thousand figure nearby.
- GT is used before it is expanded to genotype.
- The Auto / Haploid / Diploid control is described only in Settings and never appears in the six-step Procedure.
- The command line's `--filter` grammar is never reconciled with the Search Builder grammar taught earlier.
- The BUNDLE path in the terminal example cannot be typed or substituted by a reader who has never opened a terminal.
- The `bcftools view -H ... | wc -l` command is never explained as a row-counting line.
- The chapter's "Next" link jumps from chapter 02 to chapter 04 with no note about chapter 03.
- The two similar two-segment controls introduced back to back cannot be told apart on screen.
- The 852 shared positions cannot be reconciled with the total row counts given for each track.
- The caller's own "verdict" on a row is never defined as a pass or fail label.
- VoiceOver is never identified as the built-in macOS screen reader.
- The three population-database frequency keys are never named.
- The coordinate range in the worked example is never stated as relative to the slice or genomic.
- Zooming the viewport is never explained in this chapter, though the Procedure tells the reader to do it.
