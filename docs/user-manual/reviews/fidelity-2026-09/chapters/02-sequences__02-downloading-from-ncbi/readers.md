## Summary

The most common failure was unglossed or unexampled terms left readers guessing, most often the command-line notes attached to nearly every Settings entry and the two mutation-filter fields, neither of which gave a literal example string. A close second was the report leaving readers with an alarm and no action, as with the silent GenBank annotation fallback and the "well below" threshold with no number attached. A third recurring pattern was structural confusion from reused labels and section framing, two settings both named "Organism," and a section titled after a command-line tool that also contains a warning dialog users need.
