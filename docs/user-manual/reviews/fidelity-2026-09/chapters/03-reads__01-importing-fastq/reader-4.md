One thing I learned. A bundle is not a copy of my FASTQ files. LGE reads through every record, measures it, repackages it, and records where it came from, which is why the sidebar shows one item for a pair of files.

One thing I still could not do. Judge whether my own run is good. I can read the nine cards now, but only Q30 comes with a number to compare against, and GC, Q20, mean length, and N50 are all left for me to guess at.

The sentence I liked most. "The bundle records all three, so six months later the reads still know what they are."
