## Summary

The most common failure across all four readers was a term or number introduced and used before it was ever explained. Metadata, BAM, N50, checksum, "usually," case-sensitivity, and the coverage scale of the sample data all stopped every reader at least once. The second most common failure was a task presented only as a terminal command with no stated in-app alternative, hitting all four readers on batch metadata edits, re-importing a skipped sample, and the entire command-line section. The third was numbers given with nothing to compare them against, such as the minimum read length that cannot be found on the summary cards, and thresholds given for Q30 but not for Mean Q, GC, or Q20.
