## Consensus

Twenty-seven rows were hit by three or more readers.

- Five or six tool names arrive in one sentence with only iVar glossed.
- The sentence describing how the platform is detected from the reads is not clear enough to follow.
- Execution profile is used with no gloss well before the term returns later.
- The 86,281 read pairs and "clinical-scale" cannot be judged as a lot or a little.
- No time estimate is given for how long the run takes.
- The path shown in the Inputs summary cannot be checked against an example.
- The 563 primers and 223 amplicons in the scheme caption cannot be judged as right or wrong.
- There is no fallback for identifying a primer scheme without the kit box in hand.
- The default scheme is not flagged as wrong for the reader's own sample until it is too late to notice.
- The stepper control is unnamed and it is unclear whether a number can also be typed.
- The five primer parameters and two Freyja skips are counted but never named.
- The AATT to A example does not say plainly how many bases were lost.
- Bootstrap workers and Apple Silicon are unexplained and it is unclear whether the reader's own Mac is affected.
- The command-line section gives no early signal that it can be skipped by app-only readers.
- "Pins" and "tool lock manifest" are new terms with no gloss.
- "Viral reconstruction workflow" is never glossed as rebuilding a genome from reads.
- Docker Desktop has no link and no way to check whether it is actually running.
- The reader cannot tell whether the Required Setup pack needs a manual check or is guaranteed present.
- Mixing platforms in one selection cannot be pictured as a scenario that would ever happen.
- Right-clicking a row to copy a command line assumes terminal familiarity the reader does not have.
- The stray colon inside the "Minimum mapped reads:." label reads as a missing word.
- The alignment track description does not say plainly that an untrimmed file also exists.
- "By index" in the consensus-coordinates sentence is not understood.
- The alignment index in the results table is not explained, including why it counts as provenance.
- "At or near zero" gives no number for judging amplicon dropout.
- "Mostly N" gives no percentage for judging an unusable consensus.
- The backslash before a space in a file path reads as a typing mistake.
