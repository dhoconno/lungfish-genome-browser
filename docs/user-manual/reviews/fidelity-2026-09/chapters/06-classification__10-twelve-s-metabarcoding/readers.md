## Consensus

Thirty-five rows were hit by three or more readers.

- The rhetorical question "So what should you do with this?" confuses rather than guides.
- HG002 is never glossed despite reappearing throughout the chapter.
- Building the demo project is not actionable, with no menu path or link given.
- The plugin pack concept is never explained, including size, cost, or network need.
- The read-processing operation needed to merge reads is never named or linked.
- MIDORI is never glossed as a public reference database.
- Nothing says which folder LGE scans for reference candidates.
- The four-figure summary line is described in prose with no example shown.
- The Inspector is never introduced, though six settings live inside it.
- The eight-plus-six control count does not add up to the three-plus-two breakdown given.
- Min Soft Clip is explained with a mismatched or absent glossary link.
- The nonzero-lead ambiguity policy is presented with no reassurance or way to change it.
- The word "channel" for recorded reassignments points nowhere a reader can look.
- No number anchors what counts as a healthy exact-match rate.
- Read orientation is presented as essential only after the Procedure, never inside it.
- The reverse-complement and single-strand-matching behaviour is underexplained.
- A known bug in the command's own help text carries no version or fix status.
- The base-for-base matching rule is stated two different ways in nearby sentences.
- "This is not the experimental-features switch" names a switch that is never explained.
- Step 1 sends readers to step 2 before they have a reference, then step 2 assumes they already do.
- The dialog's four reference columns do not match the seven columns required on the command line.
- Readers cannot tell which of two reference file types, plain FASTA or a bundle, applies to them.
- Selecting several bundles at once never says whether results stay per-sample or get pooled.
- Refs and reference records are never explained, including why one species can have several.
- The Sequence column holds an identifier rather than the DNA itself, and readers reread to catch this.
- No percent identity is given for what counts as a high-identity BLAST hit.
- Readers cannot tell how to open a result bundle in Finder to reach the unresolved-cluster FASTA.
- Raising Min Soft Clip has no accepted range or example value to try.
- ONT is used as an abbreviation before or without being expanded to Oxford Nanopore.
- Every setting ends in a command-line flag, making the section feel written for a different reader.
- Readers cannot tell whether the unresolved percent and the exact-match percent are the same figure.
- The chapter says "four other primates" then lists only three names plus "the two macaques."
- The action bar is mentioned several times but never located on screen.
- Readers who have never opened a terminal find it the only route to two required operations.
- A second acknowledged bug, in the --compress option, carries no version or fix status either.
