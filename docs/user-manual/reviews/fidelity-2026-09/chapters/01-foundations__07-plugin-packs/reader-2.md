One thing I learned: the tools are not inside the app at all, they arrive in themed groups called packs, and one of those packs is required before a project will even open.

One thing I still could not do: choose a Kraken2 database for my own Mac, because I do not know how much memory it has and the chapter never says how to find out.

The sentence I liked most: "It looks like a workflow that refuses to start with a message about a tool you have never heard of."
