## What the six false rows have in common

Five of the six are undercounts or overcounts the DRIFT pass could not have
caught, because DRIFT checked the chapter against the lock manifest and
`PluginPack.swift` while these rows turn on what the *view* renders.

Row 69 and row 81 are the same root cause. The lock's `databases` array holds
sixteen entries, but `MetagenomicsDatabaseInfo.builtInCatalog` filters it down
to thirteen and drops the three bundled sidecars on purpose. The chapter counted
the manifest and described the tab. `conda db list` printing "Metagenomics
Databases (13)" is the decisive evidence, and it disagrees with the DRIFT
"Missing" row that asked for the three human-decontamination databases to be
documented as Databases-tab items.

Row 19 and row 101 are also one cause. The Required Setup pack's card is built
from the lock's `tools` plus its `managedData`, so it carries two rows that are
reference data rather than programs and that use two status labels the chapter
never lists.

Row 39 is the mirror of the same manifest-versus-view gap. The lock pins only
Freyja for `wastewater-surveillance`, but the pack declares four more
requirements as unpinned packages, and the card shows all five.

Row 23 and row 105 are internal contradictions rather than source mismatches.
Each is refuted by a later sentence in the same chapter.
