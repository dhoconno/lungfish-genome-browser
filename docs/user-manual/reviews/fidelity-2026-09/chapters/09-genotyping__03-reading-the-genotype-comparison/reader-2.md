The one thing I learned. A blank cell in this matrix means two completely
different things depending on how many reads the sample gave, and checking
the read count first is the whole job.

samples covers, because the 13 loci are never listed.

homozygous animal is the most consequential mistake this workflow allows,
and no colour, count, or control in the window will stop you making it."
