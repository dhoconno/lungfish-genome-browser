## Consensus

Thirty-one rows were hit by three or more readers.

- Comparison surface is never glossed in plain terms before the grid is described.
- IPD-MHC and Mamu appear together with neither expanded or glossed.
- The example allele name is never broken into its parts inline.
- The Unique column name never says what is unique about the count.
- No rough fraction is given for how full a matrix should normally be.
- The one-standard-deviation flag never says roughly how many samples it catches.
- The fixed 5,000-read threshold is never reconciled with Step 3's 1,000-read threshold.
- The three QC statuses in Step 2 are never defined or tied to Step 3's differently named statuses.
- Passed Alignments is defined using the word alignment, so alignment itself stays unglossed.
- The 1,000-read and 20-alignment thresholds are never explained or judged against each other.
- The Cohort filter example gives no way to find real metadata field names and uses an unrelated sample name.
- Bw6+ and Recombinant are named as filter pills with no gloss anywhere in the chapter.
- An em rule is never named in plain terms as a long dash.
- The working workbook is never introduced or given one consistent name.
- Hidden Cells never states what makes a cell count as hidden.
- The 13 loci a sample should cover are never listed anywhere in the chapter.
- The group record's g and vertical bar are described abstractly rather than broken apart in one example.
- The command-line section never states up front that nothing earlier in the chapter depends on it.
- Manual haplotyping mode is named and dismissed with no pointer to where it might be covered.
- Exact and strict matching test name the same unexplained idea with no single term or definition.
- The Operations panel is never given a menu path or on-screen location.
- Right-clicking the column headers has no stated Control-click alternative for trackpad users.
- The MHC-B locus row never states plainly that these are locus names needing no action.
- Orders of magnitude is used without explanation and does not match the numbers it is applied to.
- The reassuring-outcome sentence never names the two verdicts it compares or what to do if they disagree.
- The two support filters mentioned in the Settings lead-in are never named until much later.
- Percent Basis gives no worked calculation for either option, leaving the setting the hardest to use.
- Pivot sheet is used with no assurance it means a standard Excel pivot table.
- The missing quality score is named as a manual-wide concept without ever being explained or moved earlier.
- gs_id is never explained anywhere in the chapter.
- The replay subcommands paragraph is a dense block of jargon with no example.
