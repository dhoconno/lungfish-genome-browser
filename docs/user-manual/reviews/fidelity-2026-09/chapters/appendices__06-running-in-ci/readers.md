Personas were a genetics sophomore fresh from introductory genetics who has never opened a terminal, a wet-lab senior with two years of bench work and no data analysis, a pre-med student reading in a second language, and an undergraduate who has used Geneious for one semester. Reader 1 raised 68 rows, reader 2 raised 65 rows, reader 3 raised 91 rows, and reader 4 raised 48 rows.
