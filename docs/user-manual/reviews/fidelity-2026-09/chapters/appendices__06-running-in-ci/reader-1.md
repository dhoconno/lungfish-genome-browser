The one thing I learned. A CI job is a fresh throwaway machine, so anything
that passes there is not depending on one person's laptop.

The one thing I still could not do. Set up an actual job, because I do not
know how to open a terminal, where the YAML file goes, or how to get
`lungfish-cli` onto a runner when the chapter says it is not on the `PATH`
and then the templates call it by its bare name anyway.

The sentence I liked most. "That disposability is the whole point, because a
job that passes on a machine nobody has touched is evidence the work does
not depend on one person's laptop."
