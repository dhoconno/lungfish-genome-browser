# Merged reader report, Running Freyja

Personas were a genetics sophomore who has never opened a terminal, a wet-lab senior with two years of pipetting and no data analysis, a pre-med student reading in a second language, and an undergraduate who has used Geneious. None had opened a terminal before, and all four hit the same wall once the Procedure section turned out to be command-line only.

## Consensus

Eighteen rows were hit by three or more readers.

- The four tool names in the opening paragraph are never glossed as the earlier chapters' classifiers.
- "Solves for the mixture" reads as unexplained algebra rather than a best-fit search.
- A few dozen differing positions out of thirty thousand is never calibrated as a tiny fraction.
- Building a reference bundle, mapping, and trimming primers are compressed into one sentence covering three chapters of prerequisite work.
- "Pins" for the fixed Freyja version is unglossed jargon.
- The barcode file's staleness cannot be judged, since no guidance says how quickly lineages change.
- The freyja update command is a dead end, named but never shown as runnable steps.
- The explanation of why Freyja is missing from the Tools menu is too long and delays the actual instruction.
- Step 1 cannot be performed because no command is shown for entering the pack's environment.
- The example filename srr36291587.trimmed.bam is never marked as a placeholder to substitute.
- The 16,779-row variants count is never marked as expected to vary rather than as a check.
- "Wall time" is never glossed.
- The listing threshold for lineages is mentioned with no number given at that point.
- The residual figure of 12.29 cannot be judged, since no threshold or typical range is given.
- SHA-256 checksum is never glossed.
- The instruction that abundances should sum to "close to 1" gives no numeric floor.
- `cat` is used with no explanation.
- The closing note about an Intel-only container arrives with unglossed terms and no statement of whether it affects the reader.
