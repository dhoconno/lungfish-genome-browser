The one thing I learned. Reads and bases can survive a trim at very different rates, and the gap between those two numbers is what tells you the trim removed tails rather than whole reads.

The one thing I still could not do. Download the two HG002 files from the GitHub page, so I never got past Before you start.

The sentence I liked most. "Trimming is never free, because every operation removes real sequence alongside the artefact, and a threshold set too aggressively removes more biology than baggage."
