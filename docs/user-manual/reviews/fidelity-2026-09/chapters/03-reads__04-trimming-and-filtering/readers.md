## Consensus

Twelve rows were hit by three or more readers.

- Six operations under Tools never listed together in one place.
- Cut Front and Cut Tail modes named but never explained, and Cut Right's name conflicts with its described behavior.
- IUPAC ambiguity codes never glossed.
- The trimmed bundle's name in the sidebar is never given.
- "Primer schemes in LGE are viral by design" leaves readers unable to tell whether the feature works on non-viral or human amplicon data.
- A one-base read in the results table reads as a bug before its explanation is reached.
- The read count 677 is spelled out in words where every other count in the chapter uses digits.
- Two survival percentages against two different denominators appear in the same sentence.
- Readers cannot tell whether their own run should reproduce the chapter's exact numbers.
- The Required Setup pack is never explained or shown to install automatically.
- The one-in-32 error rate at Threshold 15 does not follow from the one-in-a-hundred figure given for Threshold 20, with no formula shown.
- Dropping Window Size from 4 to 1 appears to contradict the Settings explanation of why 4 is the default.
