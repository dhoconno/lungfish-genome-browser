One thing I learned. A classifier can honestly answer at the genus level instead of guessing a species, and the difference between the Reads column and the Direct column is how I see that happening in the table.

One thing I still could not do. Choose a database. The chapter tells me thirteen exist and that Standard is broad while PlusPF adds fungi and protozoa, but for my own nasal swab sample I still do not know which one to install first or how much disk space I truly need.

The sentence I liked most. "When a result disagrees with what you expected, suspect the database before the sample."
