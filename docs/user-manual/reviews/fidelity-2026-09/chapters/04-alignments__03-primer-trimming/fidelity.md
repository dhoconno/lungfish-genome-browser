tree under `reviews/fidelity-2026-09/cli-help/`, the eight bundled primer
scheme manifests, `parameters.yaml`, `GLOSSARY.md`, and the author's own run
artifacts under the session scratchpad, which I re-measured rather than

and is sound (it skips secondary, supplementary, and unmapped records via the
`0x904` mask and sums `S` against `M/=/X`).

I ran no command that writes outside the scratchpad, and I did not read or
write anything under `~/Desktop/lge-docs`.

## Overturned DRIFT rows

The author overturned rows 17 and 32 and dropped row 33. All three calls

repository asserts and which the author did not test. Repeating it would have
been drift of exactly the kind this campaign removes.
