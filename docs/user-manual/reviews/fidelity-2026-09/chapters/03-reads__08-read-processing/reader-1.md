The one thing I learned: merging joins the two reads of a pair through the bases they both measured, and it makes the middle of the sequence more trustworthy because that region was read twice.

The one thing I still could not do: import a reference sequence, which the chapter requires before Orient Reads will run at all and never explains or links.

The sentence I liked most: "Trimming and filtering, covered in Trimming and Filtering, throw bases and reads away because they are untrustworthy."
