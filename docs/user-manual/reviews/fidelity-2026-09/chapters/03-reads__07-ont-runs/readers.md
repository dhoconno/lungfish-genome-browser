## Consensus

Thirteen rows were hit by three or more readers.

- Pore and electrical signal are used before either is explained.
- The failed-reads folder and its quality threshold are never named or accounted for.
- The Phred 7.9 to one-in-six conversion cannot be checked and is never judged against a normal range.
- The Error Rate rounding rule for 0.15 times a barcode length is never stated.
- The 950 versus 927 read count discrepancy is never explained.
- The base-count field's documented wrong percentage is never marked as a known limitation or bug.
- The command-line file name never matches the bundle name the Procedure produced.
- The GitHub download and three-level folder rebuild cannot be completed from the text alone.
- Docker Desktop and the plugin pack are named with no gloss and no statement of whether they matter later.
- Two barcodes on one read, inner and outer, cannot be pictured from the text alone.
- The barcode scout subsection is command-line only with no flag or statement telling window users to skip it.
- The nine summary cards and three sparkline charts are never named.
- POD5 and FAST5 files are never confirmed as safe to ignore.
