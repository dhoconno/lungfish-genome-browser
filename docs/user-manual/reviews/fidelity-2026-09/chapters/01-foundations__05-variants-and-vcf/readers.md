## Summary

Most readers stumbled on the same handful of sentences rather than scattering across the chapter. The largest failure was terms used before they are defined, including pileup, bundle, viewport, amplicon, ClinVar, and the caller-specific INFO and FORMAT keys, which left readers guessing at meaning on first contact. The next largest failure was numbers given without a scale to judge them against, including QUAL values, SB, mean depth, and the CSI length threshold, so readers could follow the rule but not apply it to the example in front of them.
