The one thing I learned. A dot in the FILTER column does not mean the variant passed, it means nobody checked, and that is the opposite of what I would have assumed.

The one thing I still could not do. Look at a variant row of my own and decide whether to trust it, because I cannot judge QUAL when the scale changes between callers and I do not know what depth to aim for.

The sentence I liked most. "Read `.` as 'unjudged' and go and look at `QUAL` and `DP` yourself."
