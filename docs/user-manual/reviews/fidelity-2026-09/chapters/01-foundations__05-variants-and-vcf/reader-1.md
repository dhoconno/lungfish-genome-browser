The one thing I learned. A bare dot in the FILTER column means nobody judged the row, which is a completely different thing from the row passing, and I would have read the two the same way before this chapter.

The one thing I still could not do. Open the Variants tab on my own, because the chapter never says how a variant track gets into the project sidebar before the click it asks me to make.

The sentence I liked most. "Read `.` as "unjudged" and go and look at `QUAL` and `DP` yourself."
