- The SRA download's size and time are never given.
- Viewport is used throughout with no definition.
- The line about both outcomes happening during writing does not say what the two outcomes are.
- The angle-bracket and quote placeholder in the popover title reads as literal characters.
- No expected duration is given for the waiting phase of a BLAST job.
- The Reads to submit label carries a stray colon before its period.

- The meaning of one dot in the ten-dot bar is unclear when the read count does not divide evenly.
- The 99.6 percent mismatch example requires arithmetic the text could show directly.
- Low-complexity and adapter-contaminated are both unglossed at their first use.
- The two-of-three worked example is never reconciled with the chapter's own warning about small samples.
- Calling this feature does not scale never states the concrete limit.
- Long tail is never glossed, and no typical threshold value is given.
- Action bar is never glossed or located relative to the results table.
- Nothing tells the reader how to check or fix an uncompressed FASTQ.
- 12S metabarcoding is never glossed in its only appearance.

- The warning that reads leave the Mac gives no way to judge the actual consequence.
