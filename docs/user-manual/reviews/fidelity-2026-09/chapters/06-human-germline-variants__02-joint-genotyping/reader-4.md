One thing I learned. A GVCF differs from a VCF by carrying a confidence value at
every position, which is what lets samples be compared fairly.

One thing I still could not do. Open the cohort VCF and look at it. Step 3 says
to read it and no part of the chapter says how.

The sentence I liked most. "It could mean the sample matched the reference, or
it could mean nobody looked."
