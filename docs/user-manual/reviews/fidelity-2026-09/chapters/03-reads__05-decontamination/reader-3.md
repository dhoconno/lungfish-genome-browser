One thing I learned. Removing duplicate reads is wrong for amplicon data, because in that protocol identical reads are the intended product and not an error, and I would have removed them without thinking.

One thing I still could not do. I could not import the FASTQ files as one bundle, because that step is only a link to another chapter, so I could not reach step 1 of the procedure at all.

The sentence I liked most. "Two runs of one operation on two samples span almost the whole possible range, which is why a removal rate only means something once you know what the sample was."
