## Consensus

Twenty-three rows were hit by three or more readers.

- The `.lungfishfastq` bundle is never shown to be a folder rather than a file.
- "Managed" is never glossed at its first use in the reference table.
- Spike-in, vector, and carrier genome all appear in one table cell with no gloss.
- Readers cannot tell whether they need their own Deacon index or should simply leave the Database row alone.
- The 99.84 percent removed figure requires subtracting the 0.158 percent retained figure by hand.
- The threshold log line and the provenance record give different names and numbers for what should be the same setting.
- The chr20 custom-reference row in the results table is not marked as a deliberate mistake until after the table.
- Pixels as a unit of distance on the flowcell is never explained before its first use.
- Patterned flowcell is never glossed, and readers have no way to check their own instrument.
- The terminal command in Before You Start is not marked optional the way the later command-line section is.
- The `--database-id` value never appears by that name in the window section it corresponds to.
- The unused command-line flag is not clearly marked as the only one kept for backward compatibility.
- An empty settings pane is not stated to be normal and expected.
- The `Analyses/<tool>-<timestamp>/` placeholder gives no real example folder name.
- The 20 percent threshold is not tied to which operation it belongs to or how to act on it.
- Amplicon is used repeatedly but never glossed.
- The K-mer default of 31 gives no order of magnitude or allowed range for the reader to judge against.
- The Window default gives no concrete number for what counts as a short read.
- "One operation is an exception to the word removal" is hard to parse on first reading.
- Readers cannot tell whether a reference is something they supply or something LGE ships.
- The repeating three-letter unit logic needs a worked example to land.
- Variant caller is used but never glossed or linked.
- Coverage is used but never glossed anywhere in the chapter.
