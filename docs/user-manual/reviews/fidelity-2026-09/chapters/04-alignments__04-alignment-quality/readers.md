## Consensus

Twenty-eight rows were hit by three or more readers.

- Phred-like scale is used with no gloss anywhere in the chapter.
- Paralogous is used with no gloss and is missing from the glossary list.
- Tool pack is named with no gloss and no statement of whether anything needs installing.
- The orange failed-quality-check count is never marked as normal or worth worrying about.
- The Inspector, View tab, Analysis section, and Filtering tab nest three or four levels deep with no single clear path.
- Old unmarked track entries going away after marking contradicts the chapter's own promise that LGE never edits the source.
- Comparing two alignment tracks is never shown or described in concrete terms.
- The MAPQ stepper running to 255 against "60 is the usual maximum" reads as a contradiction in the Settings section.
- The same MAPQ 60 versus 255 contradiction is never resolved where it first appears, in the What It Is section.
- Hide and Remove in Duplicate handling are not distinguished clearly enough to tell apart.
- The Flag Statistics arithmetic runs too many numbers and subtractions in a row to follow in prose.
- The coincidence sentence in Reading the Results gives no indication of what the reader should do with it.
- View Settings is used before it is ever introduced or located.
- Coverage breadth and the coverage curve are never located on screen.
- The On the Command Line section never states whether GUI readers may skip it.
- Replacing the input BAM with no separate output contradicts the promise that LGE never edits the source, and readers cannot tell if the GUI behaves the same way.
- The ten-to-forty read threshold gives no middle value to judge a number like twenty-five.
- Readers do not know that samtools markdup runs automatically with nothing to install.
- Readers could not download a single file from the GitHub folder link or tell the reference apart from the reads by name alone.
- Contig is used before it is ever glossed.
- Flag bits and the Flag Statistics row names such as primary and supplementary are used with no plain explanation.
- The two keep toggles in the filtered-alignment procedure are not named until a later Settings section.
- Every Settings entry ends with a command-line flag with no statement of whether GUI readers can skip it.
- The filtered-alignment procedure is written as an unnumbered paragraph, unlike the two procedures before it.
- Records versus reads is the crux of the Reading the Results section but is only explained by the arithmetic that follows it.
- The .bai and .stats.db output files are never marked as safe to leave alone.
- The MAPQ distribution is never located on screen.
- The "rate above 80%... means nothing at all went wrong" sentence reads as a warning on first pass because of its double negative.
