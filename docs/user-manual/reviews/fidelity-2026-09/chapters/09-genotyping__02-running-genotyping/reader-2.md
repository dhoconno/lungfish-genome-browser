Three lines.

The one thing I learned. A read is counted only when it matches an allele over its whole length with no mismatches, which is why about a quarter of my reads survive and why that is fine.

The one thing I still could not do. Get from my own MiSeq output folder to a run, because I cannot tell how to make `.lungfishfastq` bundles or how to get my allele FASTA into the project.

The sentence I liked most. "Merged means one report file, not pooled reads."
