One thing I learned. Base quality scores are not fixed truths, and BQSR
corrects them by treating disagreements with the reference as errors while
excluding the positions where people are already known to vary.

One thing I still could not do. Run any of the commands, because the
chapter never tells me how to open a terminal, where to be when I type, or
that `~` means my home folder.

The sentence I liked most. "Instruments are systematically optimistic or
pessimistic in patterns that depend on the machine, the run, the position
along the read, and the neighbouring bases."
