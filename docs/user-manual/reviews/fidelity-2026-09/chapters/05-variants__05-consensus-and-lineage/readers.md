## Consensus

Twenty-two rows were hit by three or more readers.

- The chapter's file name promises lineage content that the chapter itself says lives elsewhere.
- A benchmark call set is used with no gloss and no statement of whether the reader needs to obtain it.
- The three download files are named with a singular pronoun and readers cannot tell whether all three are required.
- Plugin pack and Docker Desktop are ruled out with no gloss, which reassures nobody who has not heard of them.
- The alignment track in Procedure step 1 is never named, so the first step cannot be completed from the text.
- The Consensus tab nested inside the Analysis tab cannot be pictured from the text alone.
- The command-line remark in Settings sits far from the command-line section it actually concerns.
- Consensus minimum depth gives a range and a fixture default but no concrete anchor for real data.
- The nested Inspector location for the two further filters has no click path.
- Readers cannot tell which of the two reported depth numbers, 51 or 63, the depth floor actually tests.
- Alternate is used as a noun with no gloss at first use.
- The IUPAC row's dropped N count reads as an improvement rather than a relabeling.
- The advice to compare against a prior run cannot be followed by a first-time reader.
- The command-line section never states plainly that app-only readers can skip it.
- Reading an alignment downward instead of across cannot be pictured from the text alone.
- Readers cannot tell whether an asterisk for a deletion is accepted by programs outside LGE.
- Four downstream tool names or destinations arrive in one sentence with none glossed.
- Bayesian is used with no gloss and reads as a term the reader must already know.
- The all-N alert suggests lowering the depth floor but gives no second value to try.
- Conflict between reads arrives as an unstated second cause of N after depth alone was named first.
- The claimed sevenfold increase in N count cannot be checked from the numbers as given.
- Hide high-gap sites is described with gap in one place and insertion in another with no explanation of the switch.
