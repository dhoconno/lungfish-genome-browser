Personas were a genetics sophomore who has never opened a terminal, a wet-lab senior with two years of pipetting and no data analysis experience, a pre-med student reading in a second language, and an undergraduate who has used Geneious. ## Consensus

- Nextflow and Snakemake are never plainly defined at first use, from the title onward.
- The Python Script table row, calling itself a subprocess driver with an entry point, is unreadable.
- "LGE walks the provenance chain backwards" is a metaphor none of the readers could cash out.
- minimap2, build, and BAM are all used in one clause with no gloss.
- "The build tested for this chapter" gives readers no way to check whether it is their own copy of LGE.
- The demo project and its HG002 run are assumed as already open and identified, with no path to get there.
- Viewport is used throughout without ever being glossed.
- Signature, public key, and signer are introduced with no statement of what signing proves.
- The `.lungfishref` reference bundle extension appears with no gloss and no earlier mention.
- The MINIMAP2_1 through SAMTOOLS_5 step count gives readers no way to judge whether five is normal.
- The quoted conda environment path shows a real username with no note that the reader's own name will differ.
- "Byte-copy replays of a retained selection" could not be parsed by any reader.
- The Snakemake usage comment's core count and Singularity flag are both unjudgeable and unglossed.
- The `--config` override instruction has no worked example.
- `set -euo pipefail` appears with no explanation of what it does.
- SHA-256 is never connected back to the earlier gloss of checksum.
- Executable bit, `./`, and chmod are packed into one sentence outside the terminal section.
- The validation commands are given with no statement that they require Terminal, ahead of the optional section that explains it.
- Eleven warnings cannot be judged as serious or routine.
- The CyclicGraphException explanation does not say why the flagged step is a problem for Snakemake specifically.
- The promised list of "defects reported below" cannot be found anywhere in the chapter.
- Terminal itself is explained only in the last section, after several earlier sections already use terminal vocabulary.
- The Nextflow and Snakemake version pins give no sense of what counts as too old.
- The `--from-lockfile` flag is named only to say it does not work, which confuses rather than clarifies.
- The relationship between this chapter's export and the Workflow Builder's separate export path is left open.
- samtools is used throughout with no gloss.
- The angle-bracket placeholder notation in filenames reads as literal text on first encounter.
- "Choose a location outside the project" gives no concrete example location.
- The double negative in "exists and is not a folder is refused" needs a second reading to parse.
- JSON is used with no gloss at first mention.
- The Provenance setting is named as if it were a control, though the text says it has no control at all.
- Auditor and reproducibility reviewer are never given a plain identity.
- "Sanitizing" a filename is never explained, so readers cannot predict a generated parameter name.
- "Command line" is used well before the chapter gets around to explaining it.
- The publishDir directive sentence packs several unglossed Nextflow terms into one clause.
- The docker.enabled config line contradicts the surrounding text about no containers being used, with the container gloss arriving late.
- The `-resume` command appears before the terminal section that would explain it.
- "Image digest" is never glossed even though "container" is.
- "Cluster" is never glossed and readers picture unrelated meanings from other courses.
- Readers have no pointer to where in each export target the tool version actually appears.
- Readers cannot check whether their own file qualifies for export without already knowing LGE's internal categories.
