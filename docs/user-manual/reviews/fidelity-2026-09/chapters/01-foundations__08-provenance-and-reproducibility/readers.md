Personas. Reader 1 is a sophomore with one genetics course, no lab time, and never opened a terminal. Reader 2 is a senior with two years of pipetting, no data analysis, and never opened a terminal. Reader 3 is a pre-med student, English as a second language, never opened a terminal. Reader 4 is an undergraduate who used Geneious once and never opened a terminal.

Most common failure, in three lines, no opinions added.
Every one of the four readers stalled on how a checksum is actually compared, and all four separately lost the thread of unglossed terms that arrive without a first-use gloss, among them bcftools, sidecar, table drawer, standard error, dependency set, and Cosign.
All four also hit the same structural gaps inside the Procedure and Settings sections, including the unnumbered export-folder paragraph, the two Settings fields that both map to one command-line flag, and the six export filenames listed in a single sentence.
The chapter repeatedly teaches a term or a setting in the same breath it says the reader can ignore it, which readers described as reading a passage twice to work out which half applied to them.
