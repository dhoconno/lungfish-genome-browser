# Fidelity review: 01-foundations/08-provenance-and-reproducibility

Chapter reviewed: `docs/user-manual/chapters/01-foundations/08-provenance-and-reproducibility.md`

Ground truth, in campaign order, was the Swift source under `Sources/`, the
`cli-help/` dumps, `docs/user-manual/parameters.yaml`, and the demo project's
own sidecars at `~/Desktop/lge-docs/LGE Manual Demo.lungfish`. Six live
read-only `lungfish-cli` runs and six real `provenance export` runs were made
against a copy of the chr20 reference bundle placed under the session
scratchpad, so the chapter's list of exported file names is checked against
files that actually exist rather than against source alone.

The chapter has absorbed the DRIFT Part A corrections. Every false claim DRIFT
recorded is fixed, and the two claims DRIFT marked unverifiable (`run.sh` and
`reproduce.py`) are now confirmed by real export. Twelve new findings are
recorded that DRIFT never checked. The three that matter most are the step
numbering of the bcftools chain (row 45), the "No Provenance Available" state
(row 69), which names an export alert rather than the Inspector's own empty
state, and the `bibliography` example (row 82), which errors as written.

`LUNGFISH_MANUAL_STRICT=1 bash docs/user-manual/build/scripts/lint-chapter.sh docs/user-manual/chapters/01-foundations/08-provenance-and-reproducibility.md`
