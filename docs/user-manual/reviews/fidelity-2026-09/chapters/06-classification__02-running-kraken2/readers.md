Four readers reviewed this chapter. Personas were a genetics sophomore fresh from a genetics course with no lab time, a wet-lab senior undergraduate with two years of pipetting and no data analysis, a pre-med student reading in a second language, and an undergraduate who has used Geneious. None had opened a terminal before.

## Consensus

Twenty-two rows were hit by three or more readers.

- The core assignment rule, "whichever taxon those matches will support," never says how ties or disagreements are resolved.
- "Run it as a screen" reads as a genetic screen and the surrounding question format leaves the advice unclear.
- The opening read-pair count of 86,281 never squares with the 85,199 used everywhere else.
- The RAM banner assumes readers already know their own Mac's memory.
- "Pinned build" is never glossed.
- A "hit group" cannot be pictured as a physical stretch of a read.
- Bracken is listed among settings or controls yet the reader is told not to control it.
- Betacoronavirus pandemicum as a subspecies rank is never explained before the table.
- The BLAST e-value comparison explains confidence with a term never introduced.
- The command-line section never states that it is optional for window users.
- The sliding window's width and what makes one k-mer "smaller" than another are never explained.
- Standard-8 versus Standard-16 has no rule connecting a memory size to a choice.
- "Reads off the run" collides with the sequencer-run and running-the-program senses of run.
- "Bundle" is used from Step 2 onward with no gloss.
- The 2.6-second run time is never benchmarked as normal or given a range for a larger database.
- Confidence values are never explained until the preset table appears much later.
- The Memory mapping checkbox's default state is stated in a way that contradicts itself.
- Table counts and percentages are never confirmed as reads or read pairs.
- The five-species total for Sensitive requires the reader to do arithmetic the text could have done for them.
- The three "false" positive calls are asserted with no stated method for how they were determined.
- A working threshold between "a handful" and "a few thousand" reads is never given.
- "Exits with status 64" is never explained.
