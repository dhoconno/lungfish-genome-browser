# Reader report: Running Kraken 2

Persona: senior undergraduate, two years of pipetting, never analyzed sequencing data, never opened a terminal.

| Location | What stopped me | What would have helped |

The one thing I learned: the classifier can only report organisms that are in the database you picked, so a mostly-unclassified result is a statement about the database rather than about my sample.

The one thing I still could not do: decide, on my own results, whether a taxon with a few hundred reads is real, because the chapter offers a handful and a few thousand and nothing in between.

The sentence I liked most: "It is the classifier telling you where its knowledge ran out."
