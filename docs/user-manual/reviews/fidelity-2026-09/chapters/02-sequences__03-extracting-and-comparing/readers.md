## Summary

The most common failure was unresolved arithmetic across three different coordinate systems, 1-based prose, a 0-based FASTA header, and 0-based half-open command-line ranges, that the chapter never reconciles in one place. A close second was terms introduced with no gloss at first use, including pseudogene, share sheet, and settings registry, each of which stopped every reader who met it. The third recurring pattern was missing tolerances and thresholds, such as how much wider a framed span can be or what counts as a materially different base count, which left readers unable to judge whether their own results were correct.
