## What a chapter owner should act on first

Row 30 is the one that will mislead a reader at the screen. The card renders
`%.0f`, so the manual's 248.6 is a number the app never shows, and row 35
builds a lesson on the gap it creates.

Row 58 matters next, because it contradicts the chapter's own Mean Q note
and understates the drift. Three values differ between card and report, not
one. Rows 63, 75, and 30's knock-on in 35 are smaller repairs.

Row 26 settles the open question from the brief in the registry's favour.
`supportsConfigurableOutput` is true for `refreshQCSummary`, so the picker
carries both Per Input and Grouped Result, and the Settings paragraph as
written is accurate.
