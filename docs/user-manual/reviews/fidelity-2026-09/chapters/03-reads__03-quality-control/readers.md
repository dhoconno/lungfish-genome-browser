## Summary

The most common failure across all four readers was a number or comparison given with nothing to judge it against, hitting all four on the read and base counts for the 500 kb window, the N50 definition, the two conflicting mean-quality figures, and the Q30 threshold that later readers found repeated with a different value. The second most common failure was a term used before any gloss reached the reader, hitting all four on error probability and logarithmic averaging, 3', N, library, and Oxford Nanopore, and hitting three of four on viewport and derived bundle. The third was a task or caveat, such as the Operations Panel, the optional command-line section, and the sample size behind the charts, that the chapter mentioned only after the reader needed to already know it.
