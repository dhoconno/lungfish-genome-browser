The one thing I learned is that a random subsample should look statistically like the whole run, so if my subset's summary numbers drift away from the parent's the draw went wrong.

The one thing I still could not do is get real reads out of a subset bundle as an ordinary file, because the only route given is a terminal I have never opened.

The sentence I liked most is "Finding no reads with your primer is only evidence the primer is absent if you know the search would have found it."
