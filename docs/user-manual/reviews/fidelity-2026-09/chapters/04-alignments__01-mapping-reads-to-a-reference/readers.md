## Consensus

Twenty-seven rows were hit by three or more readers.

- The confidence score is never named MAPQ at its first mention.
- "Changes the answers a little" gives no size and no pointer to the comparison table.
- The `.bai` companion index's ownership and handling are never stated plainly.
- The read pair versus read count doubling in the 45,574 figure is never explained at first use.
- "kb" is never expanded to kilobases at first use.
- The three mappers arriving in the plugin pack are never named.
- Docker Desktop is named with no gloss and no statement of whether it matters later.
- Which records count as "unwanted" in the samtools view filter step is never stated.
- A pathogen shotgun-sampling example arrives with no setup in an otherwise human-data chapter.
- Classification is named but never linked or explained.
- A corrupted or truncated download gives no error message and no way to check for one is given.
- "The file names" is misread as a noun phrase before "names" resolves as the verb.
- Records and reads are treated as interchangeable before the Flag Stats section says they differ.
- The 99.19%-of-paired-reads figure is given with no denominator.
- The MAPQ cutoff of 20 cannot be reconciled against different mappers' scaled MAPQ values.
- The example project path in the command-line section appears with no earlier instruction to create or replace it.
- The 60-MAPQ practical ceiling is stated only for two of the four mappers.
- The Secondary alignments flags are given raw with nothing to act on and no plain-language effect.
- The Platform conditional about a typed value versus a changed preset needs several rereads to resolve.
- The Supplementary checkbox and its inverse command-line flag are not stated plainly in one pass.
- No specific coverage-depth target figure is given for what a human genome project aims for.
- The coverage-depth floor is written with its unit dropped, unlike the same figure elsewhere in the chapter.
- Coverage depth and coverage breadth are confused, or depth is used before its definition arrives.
- The sidecar file's location and how to open it are never stated.
- Whether the wizard's guessed choice persists for the rest of the session is never stated.
- The Median MAPQ column is unexplained before the table and its source for a reader's own run is never named.
- The processor-thread default gives readers no way to check it is correct for their own machine.
