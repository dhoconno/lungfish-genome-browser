## Notes for the author

Row 27 is the one that matters, and it is worth flagging beyond its
unverifiable verdict. The chapter asserts a contrast, that the download reports
in the Download Center "rather than the Operations Panel", and the code offers
no such distinction to draw. `DownloadCenter` is one line, a typealias onto
`OperationCenter`, so the two names in that sentence refer to the same object.
The only window built from it is titled "Operations". The `sra-runs-pane`
caption in the front matter is unaffected, but the sentence at line 81 and the
`download-center` glossary reference both rest on this. It cannot be settled
from source, because a toolbar surface could still present the same store under
a different name, so it needs one look at the running Preview app.

Row 15 and row 21 are both layout claims, and they share a cause. The
Import Accessions button and the query field live in two separate cards, and the
pane stacks the accessory card first. A reader following step 1 looks below the
field and finds nothing. Row 21 is the same kind of miss from the other
direction, since the chapter names one primary button in a window that has two
buttons reading Search at the moment the reader is told to watch one of them
change.

The numbers are in unusually good shape. Every one of the fourteen quoted
figures reproduced, including the two byte counts to the digit, the 238-run
study, the 244 search hits, and the 34,964,352 bases, which also checks out
arithmetically against 115,776 pairs of 151-base reads. The three code blocks
reproduce line for line against live output.
