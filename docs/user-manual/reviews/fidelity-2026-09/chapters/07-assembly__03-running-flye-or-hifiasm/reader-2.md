Three closing lines.

One thing I learned: a long read helps because it can span a repeated stretch of sequence and reach unique sequence on both sides, which is why long-read assemblies come out in a few big pieces.

One thing I still could not do: get the fixture files. I have never used GitHub, and downloading a whole repository as a ZIP to find two files inside it is not something I could have done from this text with any confidence.

The sentence I liked most: "A contig is a hypothesis about what the genome looks like, assembled from reads that carry their own errors, and nothing in the assembly checked it against an external truth."
