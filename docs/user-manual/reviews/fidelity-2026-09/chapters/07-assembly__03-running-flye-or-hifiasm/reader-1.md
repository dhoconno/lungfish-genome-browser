One thing I learned. A repeat creates a fork in the assembly graph, and a read long enough to span the repeat resolves it, which is why long reads give a handful of long contigs.

One thing I still could not do. Fix or interpret the doubled hifiasm contig, since the chapter says to collapse it by hand and never says how.

The sentence I liked most. "A contig is a hypothesis about what the genome looks like, assembled from reads that carry their own errors, and nothing in the assembly checked it against an external truth."
