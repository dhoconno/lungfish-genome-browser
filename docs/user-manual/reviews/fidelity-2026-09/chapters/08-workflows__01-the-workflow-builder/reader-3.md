Three closing lines.

The one thing I learned: a step can remove almost all of your data and still
be working correctly, so the counts have to be read against what you were
looking for.

The one thing I still could not do: judge whether my own run went well,
because I never learned what a normal starting read count is or whether 106
surviving reads is a usable result.

The sentence I liked most: "Keep this node in a chain when you are hunting
for something that is not the host, and take it out when the host is your
subject."
