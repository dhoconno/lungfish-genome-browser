One thing I learned. The coverage curve and the depth numbers are computed from every read even when the viewport is only drawing a sample of them, so a sampled window still tells the truth about depth.

One thing I still could not do. Reproduce the numbers in Reading the results from my own screen, because most of them come from measurements the viewport does not show.

The sentence I liked most. "Silence from a caller looks the same whether the region matched the reference perfectly or was never sequenced at all."
