Personas were a genetics sophomore who has never opened a terminal, a wet-lab senior with two years of pipetting and no data analysis, a pre-med student reading in a second language, and an undergraduate who has used Geneious for a semester of Sanger assembly. None had opened a terminal before.

## Consensus

Forty-one rows were hit by three or more readers.

- The 843 plus 184 SNV and indel counts do not add up to the stated 1,026 total.
- The heterozygous to homozygous ratio is framed three different, disagreeing ways.
- Step 2 points to a nonexistent Step 0 instead of naming the Before you start section.
- The mean QUAL of 903 has no stated scale or acceptable range to judge it against.
- The default of 30.0 for stand-call-conf has no stated relationship to the QUAL scale.
- The mapping step readers must complete first is a single clause pointing at another chapter with no settings or time estimate.
- Managed software environment is never glossed.
- The ten-subcommand GATK toolchain is offered as a benefit readers cannot judge.
- The gap between the 38 depth at variant sites and the 44.7 window mean is never confirmed as expected.
- SQLite is mentioned once with no gloss and worried readers into thinking they must manage a file.
- The broken phased-route --threads setting is documented in full before readers learn it does not work.
- Show Experimental Features is buried mid-overview, not placed in Before you start, and its risk is unexplained.
- The 961-record figure for a half-megabase window is given before any sense of what a normal count looks like.
- Minimum Allele Frequency's default of 0.05 is given without ever confirming it has no effect on the run.
- Readers cannot tell where to check SM:HG002, or whether they need to.
- The sequence-dictionary warning never confirms whether the GUI route needs the same fix.
- The conda install command and its failure sit in Before you start as if equal to the GUI route.
