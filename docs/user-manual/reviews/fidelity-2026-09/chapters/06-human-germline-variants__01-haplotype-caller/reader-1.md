Three closing lines.

One thing I learned. HaplotypeCaller rebuilds the sequence in a messy stretch from the reads rather than judging one position at a time, which is why it does better on insertions and deletions.

One thing I still could not do. I could not get from the end of Before you start to a runnable state, because mapping, the BAM's location, and the sequence dictionary step all live somewhere else or in a terminal I have never used.

The sentence I liked most. "On a fresh patient sample you can only ask whether the output looks plausible."
