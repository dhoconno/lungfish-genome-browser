# Reader 2 report, HaplotypeCaller

Persona. Senior, two years of wet-lab pipetting, library prep and PCR are second nature, no data analysis of any kind, never opened a terminal.

| Location | Issue | Suggested fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is, "reference genome" | The BAM sentence says reads "landed on a reference genome" but the chapter never says what a reference genome is. I know what a genome is. I do not know whose genome this is or where it comes from. | Gloss reference genome at first use as one agreed-on sequence for the species that everything else is compared against. |
| What it is, "counts the bases stacked above it" | I had to read this twice. I could not picture what is stacked above what until I guessed it means reads piled on one coordinate. | Say reads overlapping that position instead of bases stacked above it. |
| What it is, "throws away the original alignment across that stretch" | Alarming on first read. I thought data was being deleted from my file. | Add that the original BAM is not modified and this happens only in memory. |
| What it is, "reads look unsettled" | Unsettled is not a word I can act on. I cannot tell what the caller is actually detecting. | Say where the reads disagree with each other. |
| What it is, "managed software environment" | Not explained. I do not know whether this is on my Mac, in the cloud, or something I have to set up. | Add half a sentence saying it is a private folder of tools LGE installs on your own machine. |
| What it is, "the ten-subcommand GATK toolchain" | Ten subcommands means nothing to me and I have never used a subcommand. This is offered as a benefit but I cannot judge it. | Drop the count here or say it gives you nine related GATK steps covered later in the chapter. |
| What it is, "Show Experimental Features in Settings > Advanced" | This is the first thing I actually have to do, and it is a one-line aside in the middle of a concept section, not in Before you start. I nearly missed it. | Move this line to Before you start. |
| What it is, "genotype them together later" | Genotype used as a verb, undefined here. I only know genotype as a noun from genetics class. | Gloss it as combining several samples so the caller decides their variants side by side. |
| Why you would do this, "a half-megabase window" | I know a megabase is a million bases, but I cannot tell whether half of one is a big or small piece of a chromosome. | Add that this is roughly a five-hundredth of chromosome 20. |
| Why you would do this, "it holds 961 records" | Record is not defined. I could not tell whether one record is one variant or one line of something else. | Say 961 variants. |
| Before you start, "Import the FASTA ... and the two FASTQ files the same way" | FASTA and FASTQ are never explained and are one letter apart. I have made libraries but I have never handled either file myself. | Gloss FASTA as the reference sequence and FASTQ as the raw reads with their quality scores. |
| Before you start, "map them with Tools > Mapping > minimap2..." | This is the biggest gap. The whole procedure depends on an alignment I have to make in another chapter, and I am not told what settings to use or how long it takes. I would leave here and might not come back. | Add the two or three choices to make in that dialog, or say to accept every default. |
| Before you start, "a reference bundle carrying an alignment track" | Bundle and track are both new and both linked, but I read straight through and still could not picture the relationship. | Add one sentence saying a bundle is the reference plus everything derived from it, and a track is one such derived layer. |
| Before you start, "Three things about the alignment matter ... two of them will stop a run cold" | Which two? I counted read group and dictionary as the stoppers by elimination, and I had to read the paragraph twice to do it. | Name the two outright. |
| Before you start, "reading SM:HG002 on this fixture" | I do not know where I would look to read this, or whether I need to. | Say you do not need to check this, LGE's mapper always sets it. |
| Before you start, "the fix is the one command shown in the command-line section below" | The command-line section says it is optional and that nothing needs a command. So a required fix lives inside an optional section. I do not know if the GUI route hits this or not. | State plainly whether the GUI route needs the .dict file, and if so put the command here. |
| Before you start, "shotgun rather than amplicon data" | Shotgun is undefined. I know amplicon well from PCR, so I half-guessed, but I could not confirm which one my own data would be. | Gloss shotgun as reads from randomly sheared DNA. |
| Before you start, "install the underlying tool directly with lungfish-cli conda install gatk4 --env gatk-core" | I have never opened a terminal. I do not know where to type this, and the sentence offers it as an equal alternative to a menu item. | Mark the Plugin Manager route as the one for GUI users and say the other needs the terminal. |
| Step 1 heading, "on an analysis-ready track" | Analysis-ready is used in the heading before it is defined in the paragraph below. | Define it before the heading uses it, or drop it from the heading. |
| Step 1, "a sorted and indexed alignment" | Sorted and indexed are both undefined, and I cannot tell whether these are things I have to do or things already done for me. | Add that LGE's mapper always produces both, so nothing extra is needed. |
| Step 2, "Step 0's Plugin Manager route" | There is no Step 0 anywhere in the chapter. I scrolled up looking for it twice. | Point to Before you start. |
| Step 2, "a bullet, and the tool name" | I read bullet as a list bullet and could not work out what that would look like in a name field. | Say the character LGE inserts, or show the finished default name. |
| Step 2, "GATK decides which alleles to emit from its own genotype likelihoods" | Genotype likelihood is undefined and this is the reason given for the whole confusing Thresholds section, so I cannot follow the reasoning. | Gloss it as a score for how well each possible genotype explains the reads. |
| Step 2, "Both fields are drawn for every caller ... and neither reaches GATK" | This is a good warning but it made me distrust the dialog generally. I could not tell whether other fields are also decorative. | Add that every other field in the dialog does take effect. |

| Step 4, "The table drawer slides up" | The step says click the bundle and the drawer appears, but I clicked the bundle in Step 1 already and no drawer was mentioned. I do not know what actually triggers it. | Say the drawer opens when a variant track exists. |
| Step 5, "This is a defect, not a setting you can change." | I appreciate the honesty, but the section is a Procedure step that instructs me to do nothing. I expected to perform something. | Move this to a note after the Procedure. |
| Settings, "The GATK HaplotypeCaller entry and the GATK + WhatsHap Phased entry draw the same five controls" | The dialog described in Step 2 seemed to have four sections and I counted controls differently. I could not reconcile five. | List the five names in order. |
| Settings, Alignment Track, "prefer a whole-genome or exome alignment over an amplicon one" | Exome is undefined. Also this is advice about data I do not have, in the middle of a fixture walkthrough. | Gloss exome as the protein-coding parts only. |
| Settings, Alignment Track, "On the command line this is --bam" | Every Settings paragraph ends with a command-line flag or the note that there is none. For a GUI reader these endings are noise and they made the paragraphs longer than they needed to be. | Put the flag in a trailing parenthesis so the eye can skip it. |
| Settings, Minimum Allele Frequency, "The default is 0.05" | The number is given but the campaign rule I would expect, saying what the number means before saying what a good value is, is not met. I do not know what a 0.05 allele frequency floor would have done if it worked. | Say allele frequency is the share of reads carrying the alternate base, then give the default. |
| Settings, --ploidy, "a region like the male X chromosome outside the pseudoautosomal regions" | Pseudoautosomal is undefined and this is the hardest sentence in the chapter for me. | Gloss it, or cut the clause and say most of the male X. |
| Settings, --pcr-indel-model, "CONSERVATIVE, which suits an ordinary PCR-based library" | This is the one place my own knowledge helped, but I still could not tell which of my libraries counts as PCR-free. | Add that PCR-free means no amplification step during library prep. |
| Settings, --stand-call-conf, "The default is 30.0" | No units and no sense of scale. Later the chapter says mean QUAL is 903 and strong calls run into thousands, which makes 30 sound almost meaningless. I cannot judge raising or lowering it. | Say 30 is a low bar most real calls clear easily. |
| Settings, --pair-hmm-threads, "the read-to-haplotype scoring step" | Haplotype is in the glossary list but never explained in the body, and the whole tool is named after it. | Gloss haplotype in What it is, since the tool is named for it. |
| Settings, --execute, "the command is only constructed and printed" | Printed where? I pictured a printer for a second. | Say shown in the terminal. |
| Settings, --threads, "It does not work in this release." | A setting documented across five sentences that then says it does nothing. I read it three times to be sure I had understood. | Lead with the fact that it is broken. |
| Reading the results, "1,026 rows, of which 843 are SNVs ... and 184 are indels" | 843 plus 184 is 1,027, not 1,026. I checked twice because I assumed I had made the mistake. | Reconcile the counts, or say why a row can be both or neither. |
| Reading the results, "589 rows read 0/1 ... 418 read 1/1 ... 19 read 1/2" | 589 plus 418 plus 19 is 1,026, so this one adds up, but by then I did not trust the numbers. Also 0/1 and 1/1 notation is never introduced before it is used. | Introduce the slash notation once before the counts. |
| Reading the results, "Roughly three heterozygous sites for every two homozygous ones" | 589 against 418 is closer to seven to five, and What good looks like says heterozygous should be near half. Three different framings of the same ratio in one chapter. | Pick one framing and use it in both sections. |
| Reading the results, "The mean QUAL across this fixture is 903" | A mean of 903 with a threshold at 30 and a first row at 2175.06. I have no idea what range is normal, so no single one of these numbers helps me judge a run. | Give the range or say most calls fall between X and Y. |
| Reading the results, "a mean of 44.7 across the whole aligned window" | Two means, 38 and 44.7, and I could not work out why the variant sites should be thinner than the window. It reads like a problem. | Say why called positions average lower, or drop the second number. |
| Reading the results, "48,057 rows, because 46,858 of them are reference blocks ... Only 1,199 rows carry a real alternate allele" | 46,858 plus 1,199 is 48,057, fine, but 1,199 alternate rows here versus 1,026 in the plain VCF is unexplained, and I could not tell which is the real answer. | Say why the GVCF carries more alternate rows than the genotyped VCF. |
| Reading the results, "the upright bar says WhatsHap worked out which copy of the chromosome each allele sits on" | Nice sentence, but WhatsHap itself is never introduced. It appears first as a tool name in Step 5 with no gloss. | Gloss WhatsHap at first mention as the phasing tool. |
| Reading the results, "373 of the 589 heterozygous calls came back phased, 188 as 0|1 and 185 as 1|0" | 188 plus 185 is 373, and 373 plus 216 is 589, so this holds. But I cannot judge whether 373 out of 589 is a good phasing rate. | Say what fraction phased is typical for this read length. |

One thing I learned. Local reassembly is why HaplotypeCaller is slower and better at indels than a caller that judges one position at a time.

One thing I still could not do. Get from my downloaded FASTQ files to an alignment track, because the mapping step is handed to another chapter with no settings and no time estimate, and everything after Step 1 depends on it.

The sentence I liked most. "On a fresh patient sample you can only ask whether the output looks plausible."
