# Merged reader report, Extracting Contigs

Four readers reviewed this chapter. Personas were a genetics sophomore who has never opened a terminal, a wet-lab senior with two years of pipetting and no data analysis experience, a pre-med student reading in a second language, and an undergraduate who has used Geneious and expects a dialog for every operation. None had opened a terminal before.

## Consensus

- Assembler is never glossed at first use, and MEGAHIT and SPAdes both arrive as if already known.
- The 4% leftover fragment share has no rule for judging whether it is normal.
- The uncompressed-FASTA aside states a difference with no stated consequence.
- The rhetorical question "So what should you do with this?" reads as a missed instruction rather than a transition.
- Depth and variant call are used to explain the muddying problem before either term is defined.
- The SPAdes-titled link contradicts the instruction to use the MEGAHIT run.
- The "no Assemblies folder" sentence corrects an expectation nobody had.
- Rank and GC percent are unglossed table columns with no sense of a normal value.
- The "no coverage column" sentence denies something before coverage is ever explained.
- Materialize is undefined app jargon.
- BLAST is never explained anywhere in the chapter.
- The Operations Panel is never introduced, so readers cannot tell if opening it is required.
- Command-line-only settings run on without a heading separating them from window settings.
- The SPAdes-style bundle-name example does not match the chapter's own MEGAHIT run.
- The observed 44.3% GC value has no expected value to compare against.
- The warning about moving or renaming the source assembly never says what breaks or how to fix it.
- The circular-genome sentence explaining the extra 142 bases is dense enough to need two readings.
- Coverage and "patchy" are never given a number a reader can check.
- The command-line section never states up front that it assumes prior terminal experience.
- The Create Bundle button's Unknown-assembler behavior contradicts an earlier claim and is buried in the optional section.
- Read is never glossed even though nearby terms are.
- The FASTA index is described without saying it is a small automatic companion file.
- MEGAHIT is named with no introduction the first time it appears.
- "Too distant to map against comfortably" carries no threshold or example.
- Translate is ambiguous between protein translation and moving the on-screen view.
- The human-mito download instruction tells readers to do something GitHub cannot do before correcting itself.
- "Quickly" gives no rough time for the extraction step to finish.
- Extract Sequence's sub-range cannot be performed from the text alone.
- MAFFT and multiple sequence alignment arrive unexplained in the same clause.
- The BLAST 50-sequence cap is not clearly scoped to BLAST alone.
- The --assembly flag block sits inside the window-focused Settings section with no heading to mark it as terminal-only.
- Standard output is never glossed for a reader who has never opened a terminal.
- The Derived Subset metadata block is never located within the window.
- The project-root error message names a problem without naming its fix.
- The 96% dominant-contig figure has no stated cutoff.
- Standard output and standard error are used without explanation or reason to care about the distinction.
