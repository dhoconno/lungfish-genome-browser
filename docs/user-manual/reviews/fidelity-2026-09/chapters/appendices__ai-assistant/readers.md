## Consensus

Twenty-six rows were hit by three or more readers.

- SPAdes, HG002, Kraken 2, and assembly all arrive clustered in one sentence with no gloss.
- Active viewer is never defined as a window, tab, or panel.
- Bundle is the appendix's most-used noun and is never defined in it.
- The variant and sample tables are named before the table drawer that holds them is ever located.
- The chromosome 20 slice is never given a size or clear definition as a region.
- The cost of a single AI question is never given, so readers cannot judge whether to continue.
- The twenty-minute account-and-key setup has no real instructions, including that it needs a credit card.
- The key-field indicator is never explained, so readers cannot tell whether their key was accepted.
- The count of eleven lookups cannot be checked against the run-on prose description.
- The budget of eight lookup rounds cannot be judged as generous or tight.
- Grounded is used as the section's key technical term without a gloss at first use.
- IUPAC ambiguity motif and regular expression are both unglossed in a section marked skippable.
- The spinning indicator gives no sense of how long is normal to wait.
- Read bundles, analysis outputs, and reference bundles pile up unglossed and their counts are unjudgeable.
- The Azure AI-hosted endpoint entry never says whether it applies to an ordinary reader.
- A command-line flag appears inside a Settings entry instead of the optional command-line section.
- Readers cannot tell what to do when the model picker shows a Custom entry.
- The default-provider fallback order is stated as an abstract rule rather than a worked example.
- The table drawer still cannot be opened by the time Reading the results asks readers to check it.
- The counts of available models per provider are unjudgeable and invite picking blindly.
- The command line section promises a headless procedure, then says the panel has no command-line counterpart.
- The field syntax example is unclear whether it is something to type or only an example shape.
- The fallback warning buries its most important point, that a failed request may reach two providers.
- The Operations panel is repeatedly named as a place to confirm results but never described or located.
- Nothing tells readers how to select a variant or sample row before Step 6 depends on one.
- Six chat-panel suggestions are listed as a run-on sentence with no button text to match against the screen.
