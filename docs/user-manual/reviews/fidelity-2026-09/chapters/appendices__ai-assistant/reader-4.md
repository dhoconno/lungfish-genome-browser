Three lines.

The one thing I learned. Anything the assistant says is generated text and never enters the project's provenance, so it cannot be cited.

The one thing I still could not do. Confirm that my API key was accepted, because Step 3 tells me to watch an indicator without ever saying what its states mean.

The sentence I liked most. "Nothing about the key is copied into a `.lungfish` folder, so handing a colleague a project hands them no credential of yours."
