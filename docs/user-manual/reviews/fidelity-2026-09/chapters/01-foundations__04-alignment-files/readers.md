# Merged reader report, Alignment Files

Four readers. Sophomore with one genetics course (R1), senior undergraduate with two years of pipetting (R2), pre-med student and second-language English reader (R3), undergraduate who used Geneious once (R4). All four never opened a terminal.

## Summary

The most common failure was a term or number introduced and used before it was ever defined, with SAM, 2x250, Docker Desktop, optional tags, junction, and reverse complement each stopping all four readers in the same place. The second most common failure was a number readers could not verify or place on a scale, including the 91,203 versus 91,148 row count, the D/I CIGAR table wording, the 0.62 heterozygous proportion, and the 80 percent threshold with no defined middle ground. A smaller, recurring third failure was mixed notation or unlabeled multi-figure sentences, where percentages, decimals, and several statistics arrived together and readers lost track of which figure was which.
