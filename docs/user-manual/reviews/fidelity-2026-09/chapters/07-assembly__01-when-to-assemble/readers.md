Personas were a genetics sophomore who has never opened a terminal, a wet-lab senior with two years at the bench and no data analysis experience, a pre-med student reading in a second language, and an undergraduate who has used Geneious for one semester. ## Consensus

Thirty-eight rows were hit by three or more readers.

- The bacterial contig-count anchor has no non-bacterial equivalent, even for the human mitochondrial example shown.
- Reassemble never says which bundles lack the assembly record it depends on.
- The scaffold file is raised and then dropped, with no statement of whether it needs any action.
- The run-folder timestamp format is unreadable without a gloss.
- Kilobases and megabases are used without being spelled out.
- A nonzero exit code is terminal language no reader present has ever seen.
- Nothing says where the FASTA topology header is visible inside the app.
- The 127-base and 128-base figures are never distinguished, so the arithmetic looks wrong.
- Thinned and 300-fold coverage are both unjudgeable without a comparison.
- A few percent error rate is stated with no comparison or consequence.
- Percent identity to a reference is presented as a check readers cannot yet perform.
- The locked run mode picker is never explained as disabled.
- Readers cannot tell whether their own processing steps rewrite FASTQ headers.
- The genome-size ceiling table column is unusable without a way to look up genome size.
- Selecting reads in the sidebar is the one instruction readers most needed and could not perform.
- The Plugin Manager install instructions stop before the step that matters.
- The 950 MB install size is given with no download time or disk-space guidance.
- Mapping is said to discard "most" reads with no number attached.
- Repeat is never glossed even though it is the load-bearing reason assemblies fragment.
- The expected-genome-size check recurs with no lookup or tolerance ever given.
- The four mapper names at the end of the chapter arrive with no gloss and no guidance for choosing.
- The N placeholder for an unknown base is never glossed.
- "bp" is never spelled out, though "bases" is used everywhere else.
- "Drift" and "junk" contigs are introduced as if already familiar, with no statement that they are ordinary.
- The N50 walking metaphor needs a worked example to become a usable rule.
- Readers cannot tell whether the assemblers' run-time gap should matter to their choice, or how it scales.
- GC content is reported repeatedly but never explained or given a use.
- Read pairs versus individual reads is never disambiguated for the 9,958 figure.
- The worked example answers its three numbered questions out of order, losing readers partway through.
- The FASTQ header-line mechanism that detection depends on is never shown or explained.
- "Conservative" and "contiguous" are used as technical opposites long before either is defined.
- "Pinned" is left as unglossed jargon for a fixed tool version.
- The gloss for metagenome does not cover "shotgun" as a modifier.
- Hifiasm and hifiasm are capitalised inconsistently, reading as two different tools.
- Paired-end sequencing is used as evidence well before it is ever glossed.
- Reference bundle and downstream are both left unglossed in the same load-bearing sentence.
- Readers cannot tell whether a bundle is a single file or a folder of files.
- The assembly graph metaphor reads as a chart rather than a network, and "walks that graph" cannot be pictured.
