One thing I learned. An assembly coming back as several contigs is normal, and the count is mostly telling you how hard the repeats and the coverage fought the assembler.

One thing I still could not do. Find out my genome's expected size or my sample's identity to a reference, which three separate decisions in this chapter depend on.

The sentence I liked most. "Assembly starts from nothing and asks what sequence the sample must carry for these reads to make sense at all."
