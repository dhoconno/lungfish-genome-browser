## Notes for the author

Two findings need action, and one is a style gap rather than a false claim.

Claim 71 is the substantive one. No entry point preselects a clicked
alignment track, and the chapter says so correctly in its own Settings
section while saying the opposite in Step 3. The two sentences cannot both
stand. The Settings paragraph is the accurate one.

Claim 48 overstates the quality band of the PASS rows. The point the
sentence is making, that 9028 is unusually weak for a PASS row, survives the
correction, because 4.38 still sits near the bottom of the distribution. But
"the confident rows carry 20 to 27" describes ten of twenty-seven rows, and
one of the fourteen substitutions the chapter lists by position carries 6.04.

Claim 50 is true but would read better under CONSISTENCY.md's mean-quality
rule if it named the average, since the same reads yield 20.3 under the
arithmetic mean and the rule exists precisely to stop that confusion.

Everything else holds. All four defect claims are confirmed, three of them by
direct reproduction through the CLI at this commit, and the chapter is
careful throughout to say that the reported Clair3 numbers came from outside
LGE and why. No run is presented as having succeeded through the app.

## Verdict

74 true, 2 false, 4 unverifiable.
