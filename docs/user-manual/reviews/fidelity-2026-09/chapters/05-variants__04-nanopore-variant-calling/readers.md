## Consensus

Twenty-five rows were hit by three or more readers.

- LoFreq and iVar are named with no gloss telling the reader they are the short-read callers from the earlier chapter.
- The word "fixture" is never glossed as the practice dataset the manual provides.
- Importing the FASTQ or FASTA gives no menu path, unlike every other action in the chapter.
- Docker is named with no gloss and no statement of whether it matters.
- Alignment records are not distinguished from reads before the record count is given.
- The shared Medaka and Clair3 model field is described but never shown concretely on screen.
- PromethION and R9.4.1 are never glossed, though the reader is later told to type a value built from both.
- The only route to a valid model name is a terminal command the reader cannot use.
- The samtools flag -F 2304 is given with no explanation the reader can verify.
- The --medaka-model flag under a Clair3 setting reads as a typo before its reason is given.
- The result counts, 44, 27, 17, 18, 26, and 14, cannot be reconciled into one picture.
- The Phred quality comparison between 4.38 and 20 to 27 is never explained inline.
- The plain-English translation of a Phred value arrives several paragraphs after the first Phred number is used.
- The track-id placeholder in the command-line block is never explained.
- The reader cannot judge whether 950 reads is enough coverage without seeing the arithmetic.
- The sentence about the reference carrying a rare variant reads backwards from what the reader expects.
- "Sliced down to the mitochondrial genome" is ambiguous between cutting DNA and filtering a file.
- The chapter tells the reader neither ONT caller completes a run, and readers are unsure why to keep going.
- The minimap2 aligner and its -x map-ont flag are never introduced in the window-only step where they appear.
- The coordinate system wrapping at the circular mitochondrial genome is never explained in plain terms.
- The four bundled nanopore primer schemes are hard to count from the sentence listing them.
- "Provenance" and "dropped" are used together in a way that alarms the reader about data loss.
- "Analysis-ready" is never defined, and the reader is not told how to check which track is selected.
- The near-universal and haplogroup marker positions are asserted with no named source.
- Running Clair3 outside LGE is recommended with no instructions for a reader who has never used a terminal.
