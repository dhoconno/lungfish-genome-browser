I learned that the choice of variant caller for nanopore reads depends on which basecaller produced the reads, and that the model name is not a small detail but the whole point of the chapter.

I still could not call any variants, because the chapter tells me at the start and again at the end that neither tool finishes inside the program on this version, and it never says what I should do in the window instead.

The sentence I liked most is "Counting how many identical bases went through the pore is exactly the measurement the current trace is worst at, so a run of six A bases is often read as five or seven."
