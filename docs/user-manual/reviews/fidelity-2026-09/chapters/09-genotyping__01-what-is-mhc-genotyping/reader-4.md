| What it is, "copied many times over by PCR" | PCR is used without a gloss. I know the abbreviation from lecture but the chapter glosses easier words like FASTQ and FASTA, so skipping this one felt inconsistent, and a classmate who has not taken genetics would stop here. | Gloss PCR at first use the way FASTQ is glossed. |
| What it is, "primers that sit in sequence shared by every allele" | Primer is never defined. In Geneious primers are a thing I design in a dialog, so I know the word, but the sentence also asks me to accept that a primer can sit in shared sequence and amplify the variable stretch between two of them, which is a mechanism I had to read twice. | Gloss primer at first use and say that the pair flanks the variable region. |

| What it is, "a curated library of known allele sequences" | Curated by whom, and do I have to supply this file myself. I could not tell whether LGE ships the library, downloads it, or expects me to find one. One thing I learned. A genotyping call is a yes or no match against a named catalogue sequence, not a list of differences from a reference, which is the opposite of everything I did in Geneious.

One thing I still could not do. Enable either workflow. The chapter names the Workflow Library and the Tools menu but never shows me what to click inside the library.

The sentence I liked most. "A long name is not a long allele, and it is not several calls."
