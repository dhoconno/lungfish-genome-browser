## Consensus

Twenty-nine rows were hit by three or more readers.

- No reader could judge whether 156 bases is short without a comparison to a full gene or exon length.
- The `g1` marker and its trailing digit in the allele-name example are never explained.
- MCM is never expanded anywhere in the chapter.
- The "Two copies of each chromosome" sentence reads as a fragment with no connecting word.
- The 970 allele targets figure has no denominator, so readers cannot judge whether it is large or small.
- Mode is never defined where it first appears as a dialog caption.
- The tool names and version numbers for minimap2, Savont, and BLAST arrive with no explanation of what readers need to know or do.
- The "given a reference the reads came from" sentence is read as circular and confusing by every reader who hit it.
- The bundle location is never clarified as reachable in the sidebar, on disk, or both.
- The orders-of-magnitude claim about read counts is not usable as a real check.
- The 2,109 row count in the long table cannot be reconciled against 30 samples without being told what a row represents.
- The `ok` and `lowSupport` status labels are used before being introduced as something LGE assigns.
- The provenance record location is never given, so readers cannot perform the fourth check.
- Indels are tolerated and substitutions are not, with no reason given for the difference.
- The 11 to 13 loci range is never reconciled against the 13 loci listed earlier.
- Unmerged singles are never clearly stated to contribute to calls, or not.
- The "rather than as a coordinate" contrast is never shown with an actual coordinate example.
- The usual chemistry claim about 244 bases gives no actual read length to check against.
- The thirteen locus names give no indication of which ones matter or are commonly reported.
- The click sequence for enabling a workflow inside the Workflow Library is never given.
- The three rejection categories for discarded reads do not add up to the total discarded.
- The 23.9 percent retention figure has no range that would count as normal or worrying.
- The haplotype analysis placeholder section gives no menu path or action a reader can take.
- The broad-to-specific number levels in allele names are never explained biologically.
- The curated allele library's origin and whether LGE supplies it are never stated plainly.
- Readers are told to decide between short amplicons and full-length reads with no observable test to make that decision.
- Primer is never glossed despite being central to how the assay works.
- The two-way control between Haplotype Calls and Genotype Matrix is never named as a specific control type.
- The undocumented manual haplotyping mode is named without saying whether it is safe to use.
