# Reader 2 report, appendices/primer-schemes

Persona. Senior undergraduate. Two years at the bench doing library prep and PCR. No data analysis, no terminal, ever.

| Location | Issue | Suggested fix |
|---|---|---|
| Front matter, `audience: power-user` | The chapter is labelled for a power user but the prereqs are two chapters I would have read as a beginner. I could not tell whether I was allowed to be here. | Say in the first paragraph who this appendix is for and what a reader can skip. |
| What it is, "the thing a trimming program needs" | "Trimming" is used before it is explained. I know PCR primers get sequenced along with the insert, but nobody says here that trimming means cutting those primer bases out of the reads. | Gloss trimming at first use as removing primer bases from reads. |
| What it is, "a folder that macOS shows as a single item" | I have never heard of a bundle and I could not tell how to look inside one. Later sections list files inside it, so I needed that skill. | Add one sentence saying right-click the bundle and choose Show Package Contents. |
| What it is, "the Viral Recon wizard read the bundle" | Viral Recon appears with no explanation and no link on first use. I did not know if it was a thing I had to have. | Gloss it or link it at first mention. |
| What it is, "the counts shown beside it, and the coordinates" | I read this sentence three times. Three things "can never drift apart" but the sentence is long and the subject is far from the verb. | Split into two sentences. |
| Shipped schemes, "in `Sources/LungfishApp/Resources/PrimerSchemes/`" | This is a path inside the program's source code, which I have no way to open and no reason to. It made me think I was supposed to go find it. | Drop the path or say plainly that it is internal and not something to browse. |
| Shipped schemes, "canonical accession `MN908947.3` and equivalent accession" | Canonical and equivalent are used a full sentence before accession itself is explained, and canonical is never defined here at all. One thing I learned. Primer pools alternate between two tubes so neighbouring amplicons cannot compete, which finally explained why my kit ships two master mixes.

One thing I still could not do. Find out what my own alignment calls its reference, which is the value the Import sheet will not let me leave blank.

The sentence I liked most. "The right scheme is whichever one your wet-lab protocol actually used, read off the kit box rather than picked from the menu."
