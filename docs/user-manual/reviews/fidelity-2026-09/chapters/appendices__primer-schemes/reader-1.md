The one thing I learned is that a primer scheme is just a list of where each primer lands, packaged with a record of where that list came from.

The one thing I still could not do is build a scheme of my own, because I cannot find out how my alignment spells its contig name and the Name field rules were never spelled out.

The sentence I liked most is the one saying the right scheme is whichever one your wet-lab protocol actually used, read off the kit box rather than picked from the menu.
