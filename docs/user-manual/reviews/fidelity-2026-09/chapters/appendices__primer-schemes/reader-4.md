The one thing I learned: a primer scheme bundle keeps the coordinates, the protocol name, and the record of where it came from in one item so they cannot drift apart.

The one thing I still could not do: check that the scheme I imported actually matches the reference my reads were mapped against, since every stated way to check needs a terminal.

The sentence I liked most: "The right scheme is whichever one your wet-lab protocol actually used, read off the kit box rather than picked from the menu."
