## miSeq MHC review workflow

The miSeq workflow is easier to learn and review. Setup now explains defined haplotypes, genotype-only analysis, MHC reference bundles, and FASTA references with platform-appropriate guidance. Result review adds an optional walkthrough, concise display help, visible matrix column controls, a complete haplotype legend, and expandable evidence associations.

Haplotype assignments are readable without relying on color alone: assignment names are shown in the calls surface with comfortable or compact spacing, automatic text contrast, and larger targets that follow the app's content text-size setting. Inspector labels, values, evidence, override controls, provenance, and reference summaries use the shared readable typography. Custom assignment overrides retain their deliberate acknowledgement, rationale, staging, and audit protections.

## Reference and genotyping reliability

MHC reference bundles retain complete allele sequences, haplotype definitions, configurable locus order, and analyst-friendly allele display names. Genotyping matrix and reference-sequence viewers share sequence-list actions, diagnostic/all-observed views, read-total columns, haplotype-support coloring, and numeric-prefix ordering where applicable. Workbook updates preserve the revised provenance and export behavior.
